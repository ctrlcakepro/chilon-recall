# Learning Science Notes

## Retrieval practice

Retrieval practice asks a learner to reconstruct an idea from memory before checking the source. The effort of retrieval gives the learner feedback about what is available without prompts and what still needs study. A useful session alternates short recall attempts with source checking rather than replacing the source with repeated guessing.

## Spaced review

Spaced review distributes encounters with an idea over time. The spacing creates opportunities to forget part of the material and retrieve it again. A review schedule should respond to performance: material recalled reliably can wait longer, while weak material should return sooner.

## Combining the methods

Spacing controls when practice happens; retrieval practice controls what the learner does during practice. They are related but not interchangeable. A learner can space passive rereading, or retrieve repeatedly in one sitting, yet neither pattern combines both mechanisms.
