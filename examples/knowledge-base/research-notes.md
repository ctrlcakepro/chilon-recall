# Source-grounded Knowledge Work

## Evidence boundaries

A source-backed answer should distinguish what a document states from the interpretation added by the analyst. Missing evidence is not negative evidence, and a highly ranked passage does not prove that the rest of a collection agrees with it.

## Triangulation

Triangulation compares claims across independent sources, methods, or time periods. Agreement can increase confidence, while disagreement should remain visible until its cause is understood. The goal is not to average away conflict but to identify which assumptions and measurements differ.

## Reusable notes

Reusable notes record the claim, its source, the scope in which it holds, and any unresolved questions. This structure makes later synthesis faster and reduces the risk that an explanation is remembered as if it were a source fact.
